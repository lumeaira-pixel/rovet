The Craft

Woven slow.
Worn hard.

We work with a single family‑run mill in northern Portugal. Every meter of cotton is spun, knit, dyed, and finished under one roof — a process that takes 40% longer than the industry standard, and it shows.

Organic Supima

Grown in California and Texas under organic certification — 35% longer staple length than standard cotton.

Enzyme wash

A gentle bio‑wash softens the hand without chemicals, so the tee feels broken‑in from the first wear.

Flat‑lock seams

Reinforced shoulders and side‑seams. Zero twisting, zero ride‑up, zero regrets after 100 washes.

Plastic‑free

Shipped in recycled kraft mailers with soy‑based inks. The tag doubles as a knot‑tying guide.

Our Story

Started on a
leaky dinghy.

In 2014, two friends bought a 1972 Wayfarer with a cracked hull and a set of sails older than both of them. They spent a summer patching it on the hard, sailing it at dusk, and ruining every t‑shirt they owned. This is the shirt they wished they'd had.

A decade later, Sailcloth & Co. makes four tees a year, in small numbered runs. We don't do seasons. We don't do sales. We make the same shirt better, and we make it for the people who'll actually wear it out on the water.
