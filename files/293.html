12. PREMIUM WEBSITE OPENING ANIMATION & LUXURY EFFECTS Create a stunning, cinematic luxury opening animation for the LUXORA FASHION website. The animation must play automatically when a visitor opens the website for the first time in a new browsing session. A. Luxury Intro Animation - Start with a pure black screen. - Display the LUXORA FASHION brand logo in elegant gold typography at the center. - Make the logo slowly fade in with a subtle golden glow. - Add a thin golden light beam that moves smoothly across the logo from left to right. - Reveal the tagline: “ELEVATE YOUR STYLE” with a smooth letter-by-letter animation. - Add subtle golden particles and soft cinematic lighting. - Keep the animation elegant, minimal, premium, and realistic. - After approximately 2–3 seconds, smoothly fade out the intro screen to reveal the homepage. - Transition into the homepage with a sophisticated fade and slight zoom effect. B. Hero Section Animation - Animate the main fashion banner with a slow cinematic zoom. - Reveal the headline “ELEVATE YOUR STYLE” using a smooth fade-up animation. - Make the subtitle appear slightly after the headline. - Animate the “SHOP NOW” and “EXPLORE COLLECTION” buttons with a gentle fade-in and slide-up effect. - Use subtle parallax movement on the fashion background image. C. Scroll Animations - Reveal product cards gradually as users scroll down. - Use smooth fade-in and upward movement for collection sections. - Animate product images with a subtle zoom effect on hover. - Reveal headings and promotional banners elegantly. - Use staggered animations so product cards appear one after another. D. Interactive Hover Effects - Add refined gold underline animations to navigation links. - Add subtle scale and shadow effects to product cards. - Make buttons show a sophisticated gold transition on hover. - Animate wishlist icons and cart icons with gentle micro-interactions. - Add smooth transitions when selecting product colors and sizes. E. Loading Animation - Create a premium LUXORA FASHION loading screen using the brand initials “LF”. - Use an elegant rotating gold circular outline around the initials. - Display a subtle loading progress animation. - Show the loader only when necessary, such as during initial loading or real page transitions. Never display it indefinitely. F. Technical Requirements - Implement real animations using CSS animations, transitions, and JavaScript or the framework supported by the website builder. - Use lightweight, optimized animations that do not slow down the website. - The intro animation must not replay on every small interaction or every page update. - Play the intro once per browsing session, with a replay option if practical. - Include a “Skip Intro” button for users who want to enter the store immediately. - Respect the user's reduced-motion accessibility preference and provide a static alternative. - Ensure the animation works on Android phones, iPhones, tablets, and desktop browsers. - Prevent horizontal scrolling, layout shifts, and flickering during transitions. - Keep all shopping functions accessible and ensure the animation never blocks cart, checkout, or payment functionality. FINAL ANIMATION GOAL When visitors open LUXORA FASHION, they should experience a cinematic black-and-gold luxury brand introduction, followed by a seamless transition into a premium fashion shopping website. The overall experience should feel elegant, modern, smooth, and comparable to a high-end international fashion brand. Implement the actual working animation in the website, not just a visual mockup or animation description.
