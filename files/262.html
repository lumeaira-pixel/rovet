About

A studio built on
curiosity and craft.

I'm Elena Voss — a designer with over a decade of experience shaping brands and digital products for clients across four continents. My practice sits at the intersection of editorial rigour and digital experimentation.

Every project begins with listening. I believe the best design isn't decoration — it's translation. Taking what a brand knows about itself and making it legible, resonant, and impossible to ignore.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me in the letterpress studio, collecting mid-century ephemera, or teaching a new generation of designers at the Oslo School of Architecture.
