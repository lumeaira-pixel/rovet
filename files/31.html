A

I'm Abhilash — a product designer and front-end developer with eight years spent between design studios and early-stage product teams. I care about the details most people feel but can't name: the weight of a heading, the timing of a hover, the way a page should breathe.

My practice is deliberately small. I take on a handful of engagements at a time so I can stay hands-on from the first wireframe to the last commit. Clients come to me when a product needs clarity, not more features.

03 Process

How the work
actually happens.

i.
Listen, then clarify.

Every engagement starts with a week of conversations, audits, and research. The goal isn't to design yet — it's to understand what problem is truly worth solving.

ii.
Shape the direction.

I explore three distinct directions in parallel: typography, tone, and structure. Together we commit to one — early, and with conviction.

iii.
Design in the browser.

High-fidelity work happens in code. Motion, timing, and responsiveness are designed from the start — not tacked on at the end.

iv.
Ship with care.

I stay through hand-off and post-launch iteration. The best details only appear after real people have used the thing.
