The Studio

A small practice for
large impressions.

We are not a full-service agency and we do not pretend to be. We are a focused studio of two designers and one print producer who spend our days inside the details — the exact leading of a header, the way a price aligns to the decimal, the paper stock that will catch the light in a dim dining room.

01

Food Menu Design

Complete dining menus, tasting formats and seasonal inserts — laid out to feel effortless in the hand and readable in the room.

02

Bar & Drinks Menus

Signature cocktail lists, wine books and after-hours cards — structured to guide the eye and encourage a second order.

03

Typography & Layout

Typeface selection, hierarchy, grid systems and margin logic — the structural layer that lets a menu breathe instead of crowd.

04

Production & Print

Print-ready files with bleed and stock guidance, plus editable source files — so the design outlives the first print run.

The Brief

Your vision, already decided. Ours is to honour it.

The concept, direction and content are finalised — we are not here to invent a new identity. We are here to translate a finished idea into menus that feel premium in the hand, cohesive with the room, and quietly confident on the page.

What We Look For

Designers who can listen before they draw.

A great menu is a translation, not a reinvention. The strongest collaborations start with a designer who reads the brief twice, asks the right two or three questions, and then returns something that feels inevitable — as though it could not have been done any other way.

"Bring your own eye. Leave the concept on the table. Make the details feel like they were always there."
i.

Strong design sensibility, evidenced

A portfolio that shows judgment — not just taste. Work that holds together across a room, a page and a print proof.

ii.

Hospitality, restaurant or bar experience

You understand a menu is read standing up, in low light, by a guest who is hungry. That changes everything.

iii.

Ability to execute an existing vision

Not a new concept. A finished one, honoured and extended with care, restraint and a confident point of view.

iv.

Rigour on production

Bleed, crop marks, colour profiles, linked assets — the unglamorous details that make a print run painless.

The Process

Four steps.
No surprises.

A clear, calm process built for clients who have already done the hard thinking. We move quickly because the direction is already decided — our job is the execution.

01 Intake

Brief & references

You share the concept, brand direction, references and final menu content. We read it properly and come back with a short set of sharp questions.

02 Direction

Type & layout study

A single considered direction presented as a spread — type, grid, colour and spacing — rather than three diluted options.

03 Refine

Full menu build

Both menus are built out in full, refined through two rounds of feedback, with readability checked line by line.

04 Deliver

Print & source files

Print-ready PDFs with bleed, plus organised editable source files and a short handover note on stocks and finishes.
