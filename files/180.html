02 — About

Designer,
editor,
perfectionist.

Studio

Milan, Italy

I began my practice in a two-room studio in Milan's Brera district, working exclusively with independent publishers. Twelve years later I still keep the same discipline: to design less, and mean more.

My work sits at the intersection of editorial craft and digital experience. I'm interested in the way a hairline can carry an entire brand, how a single letterform can be worth a week of revisions, and why the best projects often end up smaller than they started.

I work with a small number of clients each year — usually three to four long engagements — so that every detail gets the time it deserves.

Recognition

Type Directors Club, D&AD Wood Pencil, European Design Awards — nine honours to date.

Teaching

Visiting critic at ISIA Urbino and Politecnico di Milano — typography & identity systems.
