The Enikoru approach

Slow made.
Deeply felt.

Every Enikoru piece begins as a conversation between material and maker. We work in small batches, guided by the grain, the weight, and the way light lands on a surface.

Hand-finished

Each surface is sanded, oiled, and burnished by hand — producing a patina that deepens with every year of use.

Honest materials

Travertine from Tivoli, unlacquered brass from Lombardy, and FSC-certified European oak. Nothing hidden, nothing veneered.

Built to endure

Traditional joinery and considered engineering mean each piece is designed to outlast trends — and its first owner.

Light as material

Our lighting collection treats illumination as a sculptural element — warm, diffuse, and softly directional.
