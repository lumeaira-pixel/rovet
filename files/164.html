About

Design is how it works,
not just how it looks.

I'm Léa Moreau, a designer based between Paris and Copenhagen. For the past six years I've partnered with founders, creative directors, and cultural institutions to build identities and digital products that feel both inevitable and surprising.

My approach is rooted in research, typography, and restraint. I believe good design is a quiet act of service — it removes friction, tells the truth, and leaves room for the audience to feel something.
