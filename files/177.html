Our Journey

A Love Written
In The Stars

It began on a quiet autumn evening in Florence — a chance encounter at a tiny enoteca, where a shared bottle of Chianti turned into hours of conversation that neither of us wanted to end.

Seven years, twelve countries, and countless sunsets later, we found ourselves back in Italy. This time, on a hillside in Tuscany, with the same bottle of wine and a question that changed everything.

We invite you to witness the next chapter of our story, surrounded by the rolling vineyards and golden light that first brought us together.
