Our Story

Spring 2019

A chance encounter

At a small bookshop in Florence, Isabella reached for the same collection of Neruda's poems that Julian was holding. One smile, and the rest became history.

Summer 2021

The first "I love you"

On a rooftop in Rome, under a canopy of string lights and a sky full of stars, the words finally found their way out — softly, certainly, forever.

Winter 2024

The proposal

On a quiet snowy morning by Lake Como — the same lake where they'd first dreamed aloud about a life together — Julian knelt, and Isabella said yes before he could finish the question.
