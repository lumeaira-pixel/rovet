What we do

Every build is a
blank canvas.

From subtle OEM+ refinements to full tube-chassis race builds, we treat every project with the same obsessive attention to detail.

Performance Tuning

ECU remapping, forced induction, exhaust systems, and drivetrain upgrades. We extract every ounce of potential from your platform.

  • Dyno-proven gains
  • Custom turbo kits
  • Full exhaust fabrication

Chassis & Suspension

Coilover setups, air suspension, roll cages, and chassis bracing. We dial in handling that transforms how your car feels.

  • Corner balancing
  • Custom coilover valving
  • FIA-spec roll cages

Body & Paint

Widebody conversions, custom aero, and concours-level paintwork. Your car's visual identity, executed flawlessly.

  • Custom widebody kits
  • Carbon fiber fabrication
  • Show-quality resprays

Electronics & Diagnostics

Standalone ECUs, custom wiring looms, data logging, and advanced diagnostics. Modern tech meets old-school craftsmanship.

  • MoTeC & Haltech installs
  • Custom mil-spec looms
  • Track data acquisition

Engine Building

Blueprinted bottom ends, ported heads, and full race engine assembly. Built to survive abuse and make power reliably.

  • Forged internals
  • CNC head porting
  • Engine dyno break-in

Interior & Detail

Bespoke leatherwork, Alcantara, custom gauge clusters, and full detail work. The details you feel every time you drive.

  • Custom leather & Alcantara
  • Roll cage padding
  • Ceramic coating
