About

Design is how it works,
not just how it looks.

I'm Elias — a designer with a decade of experience shaping digital products and brand systems for companies that care about craft. My work sits at the intersection of clarity and character: interfaces that feel effortless, identities that linger.

I've partnered with startups, studios, and global teams across fintech, culture, and consumer tech. Whether it's a zero-to-one product or a brand refresh, I bring the same obsession: understand deeply, design deliberately, ship with intent.
