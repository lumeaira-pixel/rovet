About Home Foodyy

A small kitchen with a big heart, rooted in Malappuram

Home is where the flavour is

Home Foodyy started in 2020 as a humble home kitchen in Malappuram. We wanted to bring back the taste of our grandmother’s recipes — no shortcuts, no preservatives, just honest home cooking.

Every dish is prepared in small batches, using locally sourced ingredients and traditional Malabar spices. From our family to yours, we serve comfort and nostalgia on a plate.
