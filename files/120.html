About Home Foodyy

Home Foodyy started in 2020 from a small kitchen in Malappuram with one goal — to bring back the taste of home-cooked Kerala food. Every dish is prepared by our family, using traditional recipes passed down through generations.

We source local ingredients, grind our own masalas, and cook in small batches to keep the flavours authentic. Whether it's a hearty Sadhya or a quick evening snack, we treat every order like we're cooking for our own loved ones.
