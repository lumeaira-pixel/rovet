FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical repair take?

Most common repairs like screen or battery replacements are completed same-day. Board-level repairs may take 24–48 hours depending on the issue.

Do you provide warranty on repairs?

Yes, we offer warranty on eligible repairs. Warranty terms vary by repair type and parts used. Please ask our team for details.

Is my data safe during repair?

Absolutely. We follow strict privacy protocols and never access your personal data. Your device is handled with complete confidentiality.

Can I watch my phone being repaired?

Yes, we offer a live service facility at select branches where you can watch the repair process in real time.
