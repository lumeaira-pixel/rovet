About

Design is the quiet architecture of how a brand feels.

I'm Élise — an independent designer working between Paris and New York. For over a decade, I've partnered with founders, creative directors, and cultural institutions to build identities and digital experiences that feel both timeless and entirely of their moment.

My practice sits at the intersection of craft and strategy. I believe the best design work is never decorative — it's structural. It clarifies. It creates recognition, trust, and a sense of place. Whether it's a wordmark, a product interface, or a 200-page book, the question is always the same: does this feel inevitable?

I work in small, focused engagements — usually one or two projects at a time — so every detail gets the attention it deserves.
