About Jasg

A neighbourhood salon with an atelier's attention to detail.

Jasg Beauty Parlour has been a trusted name for beauty care in Valanchery for over a decade. Our team blends modern techniques with warm, unhurried service — so every visit feels less like an appointment and more like a ritual.

We work with trusted professional products, follow strict hygiene standards, and take the time to understand what suits your skin, hair and occasion. Whether it's a quick clean-up or a full bridal makeover, you'll leave feeling genuinely cared for.

Our Services

Everything you need, under one roof.

From head to toe — a complete beauty menu delivered with care, precision and premium products.

Facial Treatments

Clean-ups, fruit and gold facials, anti-aging and brightening treatments tailored to your skin type for a fresh, luminous glow.

Hair Styling

Haircuts, coloring, straightening, smoothening, blow-dry styling and nourishing hair spa to keep every strand healthy.

Makeup

Bridal, engagement, party and photoshoot makeup using long-wear, HD-friendly products that photograph beautifully.

Mehndi Art

Intricate bridal mehndi, elegant Arabic designs and quick party patterns — hand-drawn with fine, lasting detail.

Waxing

Full body, half body, underarm and leg waxing using gentle wax and rica options for smooth, comfortable results.

Nail Art

Manicure, pedicure, gel polish, extensions and custom nail art — from minimal French tips to bold statement designs.
