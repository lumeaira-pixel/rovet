A Love Written in the Stars

From a chance encounter in a quiet bookshop to a lifetime of shared dreams — this is the story of us.

Where It Began

A rainy Tuesday in October, 2019. Julian reached for the same vintage poetry collection as Eleanor — and the rest, as they say, is history.

The Journey

Six countries, countless sunsets, and one shared journal. Every adventure brought them closer to the moment they knew they were home in each other.

The Promise

On a clifftop in Santorini, as the sun melted into the Aegean, Julian asked the question. Eleanor said yes before he finished — and they've been glowing ever since.

Moments We Treasure
