Core features

Everything you need to organize your photo life

From automatic face grouping to duplicate detection, FaceFind gives you powerful tools to find any photo in seconds — all built on a privacy-first foundation.

Smart face detection

OpenCV-powered detection finds every face in your photos — even in group shots and challenging lighting conditions.

Automatic grouping

Similar faces are clustered into person groups with confidence indicators. You stay in full control — merge, split, rename, correct.

Powerful search

Search by person, date range, filename, or file type. Filter by photos with multiple faces, ungrouped faces, or processing status.

Privacy-first design

Strict user-level isolation, secure upload validation, and permanent deletion of photos and facial embeddings whenever you choose.

Duplicate detection

Perceptual hashing finds exact duplicates, near-duplicates at different resolutions, and photos taken in quick succession.

Favorites & albums

Curate favorites, build custom albums, and use bulk downloads with grid or masonry layouts for the perfect view.

A note on what our AI does

FaceFind performs three distinct operations: detection (locating faces), matching (comparing face embeddings), and clustering (grouping visually similar faces). Similarity results are statistical — they indicate visual likeness, not certainty of identity. We never claim that visually similar faces belong to the same person, and groups remain unnamed (like "Person 1") until you choose a label.

Privacy & security

Your photos. Your data. Your control.

FaceFind is built with privacy as a foundation, not an afterthought. We never train models on your photos, and every image plus its facial embeddings can be deleted at any time — permanently.

  • Strict user-level access controls

    Your photos are never visible to other users. Every request is scoped to your authenticated account.

  • Secure file-upload validation

    Strict file-type and file-size restrictions protect against malicious uploads and unvalidated content.

  • Full deletion of photos and embeddings

    Remove any photo along with its face detections and generated embeddings. Deletion is permanent and immediate.

  • No training on your data — ever

    Your photographs are never used to train models without explicit, revocable consent. No public face search. No identity lookup.
