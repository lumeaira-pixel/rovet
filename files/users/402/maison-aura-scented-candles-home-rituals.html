Our Story

We make candles the way perfume is made.

Maison Aura began as a quiet rebellion against fast, synthetic home fragrance. Our founder, a third-generation perfumer from Grasse, spent a decade in fine fragrance before returning to the family atelier to experiment with wax.

The result is a collection of candles that behave like fine perfume — built in layers, with top notes that lift, hearts that bloom, and bases that linger long after the wick is snuffed.

Every vessel is handmade by a small team of ceramicists in Provence. Nothing is mass-produced. Nothing is rushed.

The Ritual

A slower kind of luxury

Three principles guide every object we make — and the way we hope you'll use them.

Light with intention

Let the first burn last two hours. Let the wax pool reach the edge. Good things take the time they take.

Let scent breathe

Our scents unfold in waves. Give them a room with air to move through — they'll do the rest.

Keep the vessel

Our ceramic jars are made to outlive the candle. Clean the wax, and they become cups, planters, or brush holders.
