Our Journey

From a chance meeting
to forever.

It started with a spilled coffee and a shared laugh at a tiny bookshop in San Francisco. What was supposed to be a brief apology turned into a three-hour conversation about everything from poetry to mountain trails.

Seven years, countless road trips, and one very persistent rescue dog later, we're ready to make it official. We can't imagine celebrating this moment without the people who've shaped our story along the way — and that includes you.
