Our story

Rooted in place,
guided by season.

Lumore began as a conversation between two chefs who believed that the best meals are not invented — they are discovered. We work with a small circle of growers, fishers, and makers within a 50-mile radius of the city.

Our menu changes with the harvest, not the calendar. What arrives at your table is the result of patience, intuition, and a refusal to cut corners.
