Our Story

From a home oven to your table.

Bakery Industry began in 2012 with a single sourdough starter and a stubborn belief: great bread takes time. We still ferment every loaf for 24 hours, shape every croissant by hand, and bake in small batches.

Today, we're a team of seven bakers who care deeply about flavor, community, and the simple joy of a perfect pastry. Every ingredient is sourced thoughtfully, from local stone-milled flour to cultured butter.

Real ingredients. Real hands. Real bread.
