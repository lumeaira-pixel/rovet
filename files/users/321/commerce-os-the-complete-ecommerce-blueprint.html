01 — Business models

Choose your
commerce lane.

Each model carries its own rhythm, margin structure, and customer relationship. Here’s how they compare.

B2C

Business-to-Consumer

Retail sales directly to individual consumers. High volume, brand-driven, and experience-led.

Amazon, Nike.com, Zara
B2B

Business-to-Business

Transactions between businesses — manufacturers to wholesalers, with longer cycles and deeper relationships.

Alibaba, ThomasNet
C2C

Consumer-to-Consumer

Individuals selling directly to other individuals. The platform facilitates trust, payments, and logistics.

eBay, OLX, Facebook Marketplace
D2C

Direct-to-Consumer

Brands selling directly without traditional retail middlemen. Full control over margin, data, and story.

OYAK, Shopify store brands

03 — Components

Five pillars of a
store that converts.

Every successful online store is built on the same foundation. Master these, and the rest follows.

Pillar 01

Product Sourcing & Inventory

Manufacturing, wholesaling, or dropshipping — know where your product comes from and how it moves.

Pillar 02

User Experience (UX/UI)

Clean layout, mobile responsiveness, fast loading speed, and effortless navigation. Design is conversion.

Pillar 03

Payment Gateways

Secure, flexible payment options — Cards, UPI, Net Banking, PayPal, and digital wallets.

Pillar 04

Logistics & Fulfillment

Shipping partners, real-time order tracking, and inventory management that scales with you.

Pillar 05

Digital Marketing

SEO, social ads, content marketing, UGC, and influencer partnerships that compound growth.
