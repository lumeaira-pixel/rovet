Our Story

A neighbourhood grill with a big Arabian heart.

Tucked along the Koppam–Valanchery road in Valiyakunnu, Top Chicken Broast began with one obsession — getting the broast right. Not just crispy, but deeply marinated, juicy through the bone, and consistently the same every single night.

Today it's a local hangout for families and friends who come for the Alfahm, the Kuzhimanthi, and the biriyani that reviewers keep calling "flavorful" and "aromatic." The dining space is roomy, the service is friendly, and the kitchen never cuts corners.
