Our Craft

Four steps to a
perfect gift.

From first conversation to final ribbon, every detail is considered.

01

Consultation

We learn about the recipient, the occasion, and the feeling you want to create. No two hampers are alike.

02

Curating

We hand-select each item from our network of 48 artisan makers — preserves, ceramics, textiles, and rare finds.

03

Composing

Each hamper is arranged by hand in our studio, balanced for texture, colour, and scent, then wrapped in signature linen and ribbon.

04

Delivery

Dispatched the same day, hand-delivered within 24 hours in the metro area. A tracking link and a photo of the finished hamper, sent to you.
