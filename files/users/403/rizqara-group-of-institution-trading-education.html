Forex Fundamentals

Understand how the global currency market works and how currency pairs are traded.

  • Major and minor currency pairs
  • Pips, lots and spreads
  • Leverage and margin basics
⌁

Technical Analysis

Learn to read charts, identify price behaviour and develop an analytical mindset.

  • Candlestick patterns
  • Support and resistance
  • Market structure and price action
◎

Risk Management

Study ways to manage exposure and make risk-aware trading decisions.

  • Stop-loss and take-profit
  • Position sizing
  • Risk-to-reward concepts
⌘

Trading Psychology

Build habits that support patience, consistency and a disciplined trading process.

  • Emotional awareness
  • Trading journals
  • Following a trading plan
◈

Funded Account Education

Understand how third-party proprietary trading evaluations and account rules work.

  • Evaluation stages
  • Drawdown and trading rules
  • Profit-split concepts
▤

Practical Learning

Apply classroom concepts through structured exercises and market analysis.

  • Chart-marking practice
  • Strategy testing
  • Review and feedback
About Rizqara

A Structured Approach to Trading Education.

Rizqara Group of Institution is focused on helping learners develop foundational knowledge of forex trading, market analysis and risk management.

Our learning approach emphasises understanding before execution, disciplined decision-making and continuous improvement. We aim to help students understand both the opportunities and risks involved in financial markets.
