About Mara

A writer's eye. A marketer's brain.

I started in newsrooms, learned to write under deadline, then spent five years inside marketing teams watching great products get buried by forgettable copy. That's the gap I fill.

Today I work with founders and marketing leads who want content that sounds like a person — not a press release — and social strategies that build actual trust. My process is collaborative, opinionated, and always tied to a number worth moving.

When I'm not writing, I'm reading essays, over-analyzing brand campaigns, and collecting far too many notebooks.

How we work

Four steps. Zero guesswork.

A clear, low-friction process designed for busy teams — you'll always know what's happening and what's next.

01

Discover

A deep-dive call into your audience, competitors, and goals. We agree on what success actually looks like.

02

Strategize

You receive a content or social roadmap: pillars, formats, cadence, and messaging built around your positioning.

03

Create
