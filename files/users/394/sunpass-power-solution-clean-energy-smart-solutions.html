Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before making the switch to solar.

The best system depends on your energy consumption, roof space, and whether you have reliable grid access. On-grid systems are ideal for grid-connected homes looking to reduce bills, while hybrid systems offer backup during outages. Our team provides a free assessment to recommend the right fit.

An on-grid system converts sunlight into electricity through solar panels and an inverter, which is then used to power your home. Excess energy is exported to the grid, and you draw from the grid when solar generation is low. This system reduces electricity bills without requiring battery storage.

Yes, solar panels still generate electricity on cloudy days, though at reduced output. Modern panels are designed to capture diffused sunlight. On rainy days, generation is lower, but over the course of a year, Kerala's sunny climate ensures strong overall performance.

Capacity depends on your monthly electricity consumption, roof area, and budget. A typical home might need a 3kW–5kW system, while larger homes or businesses may require 10kW or more. We analyse your past electricity bills and site conditions to design the optimal system size.
