On-grid systems are ideal where the grid is stable, off-grid works best for remote locations, and hybrid systems suit homes that want backup power alongside grid connection. An on-grid system generates electricity from solar panels and feeds it into your home’s wiring through an inverter. Any excess power can be exported to the grid, and when solar isn’t producing enough, your home draws from the grid automatically.
