MASTER PROMPT — GEN-TOOLS HUB WEBSITE DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT Create a completely new, premium, AI-powered e-commerce website called GEN-TOOLS HUB, specializing in power tools, hardware, machinery, accessories, and spare parts in the UAE. 1. Design Direction Build a professional, futuristic industrial e-commerce experience with: - Premium product photography and large visual sections. - Modern layouts, clean spacing, and strong visual hierarchy. - Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile designs. - Smooth animations, subtle hover effects, and polished transitions. - Reusable UI components and consistent design rules. - A professional industrial color palette, configurable without changing the layout. Do not create a generic template website. Make it look like a premium international power-tools marketplace. 2. Homepage Layout Build the homepage in this exact order: 1. Announcement bar. 2. Header with logo, search, account, wishlist, and cart. 3. Navigation with a category mega-menu. 4. Large hero section with professional power-tool photography, headline, and shopping buttons. 5. Universal product search by name, brand, model number, SKU, or part number. 6. Visual category grid. 7. AI Tool Copilot. 8. Carbon Brush Finder. 9. Featured products and best sellers. 10. Special offers and bundle deals. 11. Brand directory. 12. Build Your Own Toolkit. 13. Shop by industry and application. 14. Spare parts and accessories. 15. Bulk orders and business quotations. 16. Buying guides and technical articles. 17. Customer support and trust information. 18. Complete footer with contact information and legal pages. 3. Product Categories Create editable categories and subcategories for: - Power Tools: drills, angle grinders, rotary hammers, impact wrenches, saws, sanders, and blowers. - Hand Tools: spanners, pliers, screwdrivers, hammers, and measuring tools. - Accessories: drill bits, cutting discs, grinding wheels, batteries, and chargers. - Hardware: screws, bolts, anchors, fasteners, and fittings. - Machinery: welding machines, compressors, generators, and cutting machines. - Safety & PPE: helmets, gloves, safety glasses, footwear, and respirators. - Tool Storage: toolboxes, bags, organizers, and workshop storage. 4. Product Listing and Detail Pages Every product card should support: - Genuine product image. - Brand and product name. - Model number and key specifications. - Price in AED. - Verified availability. - Wishlist and Add to Cart. - Quick View and Compare. Product detail pages must include a photo gallery, technical specifications, product description, included accessories, warranty information, manuals, delivery details, compatible spare parts, and related products. 5. AI Tool Copilot Create an AI-powered product discovery experience where customers describe their work and receive relevant product recommendations. Ask about the application, material, power requirements, budget, and corded or cordless preference. Display recommended products, verified specifications, prices, availability, compatibility, and comparison options. Connect AI recommendations to the actual product database. Never invent product specifications, stock, prices, or compatibility. 6. Carbon Brush Finder Build a technical search system that identifies compatible carbon brushes using machine brand, model number, brush code, or manufacturer part number. Display verified machine matches, brush part numbers, dimensions in millimeters, product images, prices, and availability. Create an administrator interface to import and manage machine-to-brush compatibility records using CSV or Excel. Clearly flag unverified matches instead of presenting uncertain compatibility as fact. 7. Build Your Own Toolkit Create a guided builder: Choose Job → Select Main Tool → Choose Compatible Battery → Choose Charger → Add Accessories → Add PPE → Review Kit → Add to Cart or Send Enquiry. Calculate totals automatically and validate product compatibility and stock. 8. Cart, Checkout and Enquiries Create a functional shopping cart and checkout system with quantity controls, stock validation, customer details, UAE delivery addresses, transparent totals, and order confirmation. Allow administrators to enable or disable: - Online payment. - WhatsApp enquiry. - Email enquiry. - Cash on Delivery, when operational. - Supported card and Apple Pay payment methods through a compatible payment provider. WhatsApp enquiries must include the correct product details, model numbers, quantities, and product links. Never store raw payment-card information or mark pending payments as paid. 9. Admin Dashboard Build a secure administration panel with: - Sales overview and analytics. - Product and category management. - Prices and inventory. - Customer and order management. - Supplier sourcing and purchase costs. - Bulk quotations and enquiries. - Carbon brush database. - AI Copilot settings. - Toolkit compatibility rules. - Payment and shipping settings. - Promotions, SEO, user roles, and audit logs. All important changes must persist in the database and be protected by server-side authorization. 10. Supplier Management Create supplier records containing company details, contact information, product availability, supplier cost, minimum order quantity, lead time, and quotation history. Provide a workflow to verify availability, calculate costs and margins, prepare customer quotations, and update inventory. Keep supplier costs and confidential business information private. 11. Mobile-First Design Create a dedicated mobile layout featuring: - Compact navigation. - Accessible product search. - Two-column product cards. - Swipeable product carousels. - Filter drawers. - Clear product specifications. - Easy-to-reach Add to Cart buttons. - Mobile-friendly AI tools and forms. - Optional bottom navigation when useful. Do not simply shrink the desktop layout. 12. Technical Requirements Use a real persistent database, secure backend APIs, authentication, role-based permissions, backups, validation, and error handling. Implement SEO-friendly URLs, page metadata, structured product data, breadcrumbs, optimized images, accessible navigation, and responsive layouts. Use real product photographs and verified product information. Do not fabricate reviews, official logos, business details, supplier contacts, or stock levels. Create editable pages for About Us, Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions, Shipping, Returns, Warranty, and Payment Information. Support AED and UAE delivery requirements. Prepare the architecture for Arabic and right-to-left layouts. 13. Implementation Strategy Build the website in phases: Phase 1: Design system, homepage, navigation, categories, and product pages. Phase 2: Database, search, filters, inventory, and admin panel. Phase 3: Cart, checkout, payment integration, orders, and WhatsApp enquiries. Phase 4: Supplier sourcing, bulk quotations, and customer accounts. Phase 5: AI Tool Copilot, Carbon Brush Finder, and Toolkit Builder. Phase 6: Security, mobile testing, SEO, performance, and launch preparation. FINAL INSTRUCTION Build a complete, professional, production-oriented GEN-TOOLS HUB e-commerce platform—not merely a visual prototype. Prioritize a polished homepage, excellent mobile UX, accurate product information, reliable purchasing workflows, and maintainable architecture. Use working integrations wherever configured. Clearly identify features that still require external credentials, verified data, or additional setup. Test all important user journeys before declaring the website ready for launch.
