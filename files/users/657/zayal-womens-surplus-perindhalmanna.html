Our Story

Rooted in Perindhalmanna, made for you.

Zayal began with a simple idea — that women in our community deserve access to beautiful, comfortable clothing without the big-city price tag. We curate surplus pieces from trusted makers, focusing on fabrics that breathe, fits that flatter, and colours that feel like home.

Every item is inspected by hand before it reaches our shelves. When you shop with us, you're not just buying a garment — you're supporting a small local business that believes in quality over quantity.
