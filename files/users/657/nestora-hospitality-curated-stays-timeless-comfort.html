The Nestora Way

Hospitality that feels less like service, more like being expected.

We don't do lobbies or front desks. Each Nestora home comes with a local host who knows the butcher, the best table at the trattoria, and exactly when to disappear. It's the difference between a hotel and a house that happens to be yours for a week.

A host, not a concierge

Real people with real recommendations. They'll stock the fridge before you arrive and leave you alone after.

Design that earns its keep

Every object chosen for a reason. Linen that softens with washing, glasses that feel right in the hand.

Time, unhurried

Check-in when you like. Breakfast whenever you wake. The only schedule is the one you don't make.

Since 2014

We started with one house and a stubborn idea.

That a place to stay could feel personal. Not themed, not branded, not a product — just a house that someone had thought about. We now look after twelve homes across six countries, each one chosen because it made us stop and stare.

We're still small on purpose. Every property is visited, every host is known to us. If it's not somewhere we'd want to spend our own week, it doesn't make the list.
