Our Story

Born from
quiet rebellion

Zaayal began in Perindhalmanna with a simple belief: women deserve clothing that feels as good as it looks — without the waste, without the markup, without the noise.

We source premium surplus fabrics from ethical mills and independent makers, then craft limited-run pieces that honour both the material and the woman wearing them.
