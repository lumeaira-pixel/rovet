Why Alfat

Where technology meets tenderness.

Founded in 2008, Alfat Hospital was built on a simple belief: that exceptional medical care should never feel cold or impersonal. Today, our 300-bed facility combines cutting-edge diagnostics with private, light-filled rooms and a team that knows your name.

  • JCI-accredited patient safety protocols
  • 24/7 on-site consultants, no waiting for specialists
  • Private suites with natural light and family space
