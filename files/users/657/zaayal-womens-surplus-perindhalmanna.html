Our Story

Born in Perindhalmanna, made for her.

Zaayal began as a quiet rebellion against fast fashion — a small, women-led studio in Perindhalmanna dedicated to giving beautiful surplus pieces a second life. We believe style should feel personal, not mass-produced.

Every garment is hand-selected, gently restored, and photographed in natural Kerala light. What you see is what you get: honest, timeless, and yours.
