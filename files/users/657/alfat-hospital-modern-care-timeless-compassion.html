Our story

A legacy of healing,
built on trust.

Since 1998, Alfat Hospital has been a cornerstone of healthcare in the community. What began as a small clinic has grown into a full-service medical center — but our founding principle remains unchanged: every patient is family.

We invest in the latest medical technology and, more importantly, in the people who use it. Our physicians are leaders in their fields, our nurses are compassionate advocates, and our facilities are designed to put you at ease.
