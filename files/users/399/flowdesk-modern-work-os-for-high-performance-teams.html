Features

Everything your team needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Stop juggling five tools. Flowdesk brings your projects, docs, and communication into one beautifully simple workspace.

Unified workspace

Projects, docs, and conversations live together. No more context-switching between tools.

AI-powered workflows

Automate repetitive tasks, summarize threads, and draft updates — all with a single prompt.

Real-time collaboration

Edit docs, boards, and plans together with live cursors, comments, and instant sync.

Insightful analytics

Async communication

Threads, voice notes, and rich docs let your team collaborate across time zones.
