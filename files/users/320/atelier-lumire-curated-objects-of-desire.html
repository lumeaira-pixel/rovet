Our Story

Made slowly.
Made to last.

Atelier Lumière began in a small studio in the Cotswolds, founded on a simple conviction: that the objects we live with should be made with care, from materials that age beautifully.

Every piece passes through the hands of a single maker — from the first sketch to the final polish. We work in small runs, never more than forty of a kind, so that nothing is rushed and nothing is wasted.

The Craft

Four principles.
Zero compromise.

Honest materials

Locally sourced stoneware, oak, and brass — chosen for how they age, not how they photograph.

Single-maker

One pair of hands sees each piece from start to finish. No assembly lines, no shortcuts.

Closed loop

Every offcut is reclaimed. Every piece is repairable. Nothing leaves the studio as waste.

Built to outlast

We guarantee every piece for life. If it fails, we repair or replace it — no questions asked.
