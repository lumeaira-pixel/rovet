Our Story

About the Festival

The Al-Fathih Arts Festival is the flagship cultural event of our Islamic College, celebrating the intersection of faith and artistic expression. For over two decades, it has brought together students from across the region to compete, create, and connect through calligraphy, recitation, poetry, visual arts, and performance. This year's theme, "Light of Tradition, Voice of Tomorrow," invites participants to honour the past while shaping the future.
