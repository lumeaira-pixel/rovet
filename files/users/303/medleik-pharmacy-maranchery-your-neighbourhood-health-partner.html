About Medleik Pharmacy

A trusted neighbourhood healthcare partner in Maranchery.

We opened our doors with one simple belief: healthcare should feel approachable. Medleik Pharmacy aims to be the friendly face you can turn to for everyday medicines, wellness essentials and clear, respectful guidance — right in your own neighbourhood.

Our team listens first. We take time to explain medicine labels and prescription instructions clearly, encourage you to follow your prescriber’s directions, and always maintain privacy when handling prescriptions and personal information.

Care Beyond Medicines

Our commitment to responsible, patient-first care.

At Medleik Pharmacy, we believe good pharmacy care goes beyond the counter. We aim to create a calm, respectful space where you feel comfortable asking questions and confident about the medicines and products you take home.

Important

Our website is an information and enquiry service. It does not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Please consult a registered healthcare professional for medical concerns.

Every customer is greeted with courtesy and patience. Whether you have a quick question or need help understanding a prescription, our team is here to listen and assist.
Qualified pharmacy staff help you understand medicine labels and prescription instructions clearly. We encourage you to follow your prescriber’s directions and to ask questions whenever something is unclear.
We help you understand dosage schedules, storage instructions and expiry dates. For any warning symptoms or medicine-related concerns, we refer you to an appropriate registered healthcare professional.
