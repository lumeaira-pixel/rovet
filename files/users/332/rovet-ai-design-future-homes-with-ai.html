Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a powerful PC to take this course?
Not at all. All AI rendering workflows are cloud-based. A mid-range laptop and a stable internet connection are sufficient. We'll guide you through setting up free cloud GPU resources.
Will I receive a certificate after completion?
Yes. Every student who completes the course and submits a final project receives a verified Rovet AI Certificate of Completion, shareable on LinkedIn.
Do I get lifetime access to the course materials?
Absolutely. You get lifetime access to all video lessons, project files, prompt libraries, and future updates to the course.
What software will I need?
We primarily use Midjourney, Stable Diffusion (via web UI), D5 Render AI, and SketchUp. We provide setup guides for all of them, including free alternatives.
