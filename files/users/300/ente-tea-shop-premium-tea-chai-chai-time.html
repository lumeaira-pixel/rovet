Our Story

From a tiny tea stall to your favourite corner.

Ente Tea Shop began as a two-table stall run by our grandmother in the misty hills of Munnar. Today, we still source every leaf from the same family gardens — and we still brew every cup like it's for someone we love.

No shortcuts. No syrups. Just good leaves, good milk, and a whole lot of patience.
