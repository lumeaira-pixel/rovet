Our Core Services

Tailored regulatory, documentation, and recruitment solutions for fresh and experienced nursing professionals.

1. Licensing & Dataflow

  • Primary Source Verification: Complete Dataflow processing for Kuwait MOH, DHA, HAAD/DOH, SCFHS, OMSB, and QCHP.
  • Troubleshooting: Transfer existing reports, add experience, or resolve "Unable to Verify" (UTV) cases.
  • Exam Scheduling: Prometric, Pearson VUE, NCLEX-RN, NMC CBT, and OET/IELTS exam registration.
Inquire Licensing

2. Certificate Attestation

  • HRD & MEA: State HRD attestation and Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) Apostille.
  • Embassy Stamping: Kuwait Embassy, Saudi Embassy, and GCC country legalizations.
  • Council Services: Nursing Council renewals, state transfers, and Good Standing Certificates (GSC).
Start Attestation

3. Travel & Visa

  • Visa Stamping: Work visa processing (Kuwait Article 18, GCC, UK, Europe).
  • Medical & PCC: GAMCA/WAFID medical test appointments and Police Clearance guidance.
  • Logistics: Air ticket bookings, pre-departure briefings, and airport pickup options.
Process Visa

4. Placements

  • Direct Placements: Hospitals in India, Kuwait (Public MOH & Private Sector), UAE, Saudi, UK, Ireland, Canada & Australia.
  • Recruitment Support: End-to-end recruitment with zero-friction interview support and contract verification.
