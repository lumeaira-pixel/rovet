Our Story

A table built on relationships, not transactions.

Verdant Table began in 2012 with a simple idea: cook what the season gives you, and know every person who grew it. We work with 14 small farms within 60 miles of our kitchen, and our menu changes weekly to honor what's at its peak.

Our dining room is warm, unpretentious, and designed for lingering. No rushed seatings, no fuss—just honest food, natural wine, and the kind of evening you'll want to repeat.

Why Verdant Table

The details that make the difference.

Seed-to-plate sourcing

We name the farms on our menu because transparency matters. Every ingredient is traceable to the person who grew or caught it.

A menu that changes weekly

What's at its peak dictates what's on your plate. Expect new dishes every week and a rotating natural wine list curated by our sommelier.

Warmth over formality

No white tablecloths required. Our dining room is relaxed, our service is genuine, and our team remembers your name after the first visit.

Private events, handled

From intimate anniversary dinners to 40-person celebrations, our team builds custom menus and handles every detail so you can be present.

Low-waste kitchen

We compost, we ferment, we use the whole animal and the whole vegetable. Our kitchen sends less than 8% of its waste to landfill.

Fair pricing, always

We pay our farmers and our staff above market rate—and we price our menu so you can enjoy a full evening without a second mortgage.
