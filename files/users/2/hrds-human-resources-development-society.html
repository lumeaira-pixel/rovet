About Us

A legacy of
skill development
since 2001.

Established in October 2001 and registered under the Societies Registration Act (XXI) of 1860 (Reg. No. 687/2001), the Human Resources Development Society (HRDS) is a Non-Governmental Organization dedicated to manpower development, skill evaluation, and vocational training excellence.

Operating from its Head Office in New Delhi, HRDS works to strengthen workforce readiness, support affiliate training institutions, and promote transparent, credible skill assessment standards aligned with national skill development initiatives.

Assessment Reach

Vision

To be a trusted, leading authority in skill assessment and certification that equips learners with practical competencies for sustainable livelihoods and career growth.

Mission

To conduct fair, reliable, and industry-aligned assessments and training support that enhance employability and institutional quality.

Core Focus Areas & Objectives

What we do.

Four pillars that define our commitment to skill excellence and workforce readiness.

Skill Assessment & Certification

Conducting structured skill evaluations and issuing certifications for candidates who complete approved training programs.

Vocational & Technical Domains

Supporting skill development across engineering, healthcare/medical support, information technology, and soft skills.

Institutional Support & Standards

Assisting affiliated training centers and vocational providers in upgrading training infrastructure, facilities, and instructional quality.

Student Support & Research

Encouraging scholarships/stipends for eligible learners, alongside seminars, study tours, and research on emerging technologies.
