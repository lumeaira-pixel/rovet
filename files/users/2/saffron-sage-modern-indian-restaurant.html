Our Story

A family table,
reimagined.

Saffron & Sage began in 2016 as a Sunday supper club in a tiny kitchen — one long table, a handful of friends, and the recipes my grandmother carried from Punjab to the coast. Those suppers grew, the table got longer, and eventually we found a home in the city we love.

Today, our menu still follows the seasons and the spice box. We grind our masalas in-house, source produce from growers we know by name, and cook every dish to order — the way it should be. Chef Anaya Malhotra leads the kitchen with a simple belief: food is memory, and every plate should feel like a welcome.

Anaya Malhotra
Head Chef & Founder
