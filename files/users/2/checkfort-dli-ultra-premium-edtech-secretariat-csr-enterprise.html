Corporate & Executive Mandate

Bridging Structural Divides in Educational Technology

At Checkfort Digital Learning Infrastructure, we operate under a single mandate: institutional excellence should not be governed by capital constraints.

Originally incubated with a mission to accelerate IT-oriented education across public and aided schools, Checkfort has evolved into a full-scale educational technology engine backed by the UIS Foundation. By deploying capital through zero-based structures and CSR subvention, we eliminate the heavy setup and maintenance costs traditionally associated with school web architecture, administrative software, and modern learning infrastructure.

Project Digital@School

The EdTech & Infrastructure Suite

Four modular pillars providing complete institutional modernization, offline STEM intelligence, and sustainable campus operations.

Project NODE

Digital Secretariat & Institutional Portal

Replaces fragmented, expensive commercial school websites (typically costing ₹35,000–₹50,000) with a turnkey, high-security cloud platform.

Automated Dispatches: Centralized parent-teacher communication, attendance notifications, and digital circulars.
Alumni & Donor Registry: Portals designed to connect institutions with global alumni networks for infrastructure grants.
Managed Upkeep: Continuous cloud hosting, maintenance, and security protocols fully managed by Checkfort DLI.
Project NEXUS

Modular & Offline AI Learning Engine

Delivers advanced STEM and digital literacy capabilities to classrooms regardless of bandwidth or external internet reliability.

Edge Computing Nodes: Low-power localized hardware pre-loaded with interactive coding, robotics, and science modules.
Offline AI Literacy: Guided AI tutors and curriculum modules designed specifically for primary & secondary grades.
Performance Analytics: Dashboards tracking individual student progress without requiring constant internet.
Project KINETIC

Clean-Energy Campus Infrastructure

Modernizes campus-wide communication using sustainable, low-maintenance hardware and off-grid solar energy.

Solar-Assisted Digital Displays: Off-grid digital billboards for real-time announcements, emergency alerts, & showcases.
Low Operational Overhead: Engineered to operate seamlessly with zero grid electricity draw, mitigating utility expenses.
Project VANGUARD

Veteran-Led Character & Civic Academies

Combines digital literacy with physical discipline, military-grade ethics, and proactive civic responsibility.

Leadership & Ethics: Modules led by defense veterans focusing on civic duty, cyber-hygiene, & character building.
Disaster Preparedness: Practical physical training and emergency response drills integrated into school hours.
