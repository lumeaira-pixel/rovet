Section 1: About Us

Bridging Grassroots Innovation with International Markets

At Rural Global Connect, we believe that geographic location should never restrict business potential. Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) and rural entrepreneurs produce high-quality agricultural products, textiles, handicrafts, and engineered components—yet often lack direct pathways to global trade networks.

Our firm acts as your international trade secretariat. We handle global market entry strategy, buyer matchmaking, export documentation, compliance, and capital subsidies so regional entrepreneurs can expand beyond domestic boundaries with confidence.

Section 2: Our Services

Our Core Services

Comprehensive advisory, execution, and subsidy services designed specifically for regional Indian enterprise growth.

1. Global Market Entry & Buyer Networking

  • B2B Buyer Matchmaking: Connecting regional manufacturers directly with verified importers, wholesale buyers, and international trade agencies across the GCC, Europe, North America, and Southeast Asia.
  • International Trade Delegation Support: Organizing and facilitating your participation in international trade fairs, buyer-seller meets, and global expos.
Request Matchmaking

2. Government Incentive & Subsidy Facilitation

  • Export Promotion Mission (EPM): Guiding MSMEs through Niryat Protsahan (trade finance & subvention) and Niryat Disha (market readiness & compliance).
  • Capital & Import Subsidies: Assisting with EPCG (Export Promotion Capital Goods) for zero-duty machinery imports, RoDTEP, Duty Drawback, and ECGC export insurance.
Claim Export Subsidies

3. End-to-End Export Compliance & Logistics

  • Licensing & Documentation: Complete processing for Import Export Code (IEC), RCMC registration, phytosanitary certifications, and customs documentation.
  • Quality & International Packaging: Upgrading local product packaging, lab testing, and quality standardizations (ISO, CE, FDA) to match global buyer requirements.
Get Compliance Support
