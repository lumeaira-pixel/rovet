The Lumière Approach

Botanical intelligence, formulated with restraint.

We believe skincare should do more with less. Every ingredient in our Moisture Veil is chosen for a measurable reason — no filler, no fragrance, no false promises. Just a precise blend that supports your skin's natural rhythm.

Triple-Weight Hyaluronic Complex

Three molecular weights hydrate at different depths — surface, mid-layer, and deeper reservoir — for a plumped, cushioned feel that lasts.

Biomimetic Ceramide Barrier

Ceramides identical to those found naturally in skin reinforce the lipid barrier, reducing water loss and shielding against daily environmental stress.

Cold-Pressed Botanical Blend

Squalane from olives, calendula, and green tea extract are cold-processed to preserve their antioxidant potency and soothing properties.

Featherlight, Fast-Absorbing

A whipped gel-cream texture that disappears into skin in under 30 seconds — no tackiness, no shine, no interference with makeup or SPF.

The Daily Ritual

Three steps to a softer, steadier complexion.

A simple, repeatable practice — designed to fit into your morning and evening without fuss.

01

Cleanse

Begin with a gentle, pH-balanced cleanser to remove impurities without stripping. Pat dry — leave skin slightly damp.

02

Veil

Scoop a pearl-sized amount of Moisture Veil. Warm between fingertips and press gently into skin — upward, outward, never dragging.

03

Protect

In the morning, follow with a broad-spectrum SPF. In the evening, let the veil work overnight — wake to skin that feels cushioned and calm.
