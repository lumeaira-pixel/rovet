Three generations
of weaving trust.

What began as a small family loom in Anakkarra has grown into a beloved destination for those who seek the extraordinary — sarees that carry the weight of tradition and the lightness of modern elegance.

We work directly with master weavers across Tamil Nadu and Kerala. No middlemen, no mass production. Just pure, patient craft.
