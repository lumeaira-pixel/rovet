Our story

A studio built on attention

Lumieère began in 2012 with a simple belief: that a salon visit should feel less like an errand and more like a reset. We kept the space small, the lighting warm, and the team close-knit.

Today, our stylists specialise in everything from precision bobs to curl-specific dry cutting. But the core of what we do hasn't changed — we listen first, recommend second, and never rush the finish.

Questions

Good to know

We welcome walk-ins for quick services like blow-dries and trims, but colour and smoothing treatments need a reservation. We recommend booking at least a week ahead for colour appointments.

Come with dry, unwashed hair (day-two texture is ideal). Avoid heavy styling products and bring any reference photos. We'll handle the rest — including a full consultation before we mix colour.

Yes. We use a curated line of vegan, cruelty-free colour and care products. If you have specific sensitivities, let us know and we'll patch-test or suggest an alternative.

We ask for 24 hours' notice for cancellations. Colour appointments cancelled with less notice may incur a 50% fee, which is credited toward your next visit.
