Founder, Digishah

I'm Shahanada, a freelance digital marketer based in Calicut, Kerala. My journey started with a curiosity about how brands connect with people online — and that curiosity turned into a focused practice across social media, design, and paid advertising.

Under the brand Digishah, I work with small businesses, creators, and startups who need a clear, consistent digital presence without the overhead of a full agency. I handle the strategy, the creatives, and the campaigns — end to end.

My learning journey has been hands-on: building practice projects, experimenting with ad creatives, studying what makes content stop the scroll, and refining my graphic design skills. Every project is a chance to learn something new and apply it immediately.
