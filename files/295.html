Our philosophy

We do not teach trading.
We build market intuition.

Most courses teach patterns. Rizqara teaches you how to think about probability, price action, and risk — the way institutional desks do. Our curriculum rests on three pillars: technical precision, psychological resilience, and real-time execution under live market conditions.

Whether you are opening your first chart or refining a system you have run for years, our mentors have traded through multiple cycles and will guide you with honesty, structure, and patience.

Curriculum

A syllabus built for the real markets.

Twelve weeks of deliberate practice across six disciplines. Each module pairs theory with live charting, recorded walkthroughs, and a trade-journal review at the end of the week.

View enrollment Download syllabus
01

Market structure & price action

Timeframes, trend, impulse vs. correction, liquidity and the anatomy of a clean setup.

02

Risk, position sizing & expectancy

The math that keeps you in the game: R-multiples, drawdown control, and trade management.

03

Order flow & volume profile

Reading the auction — VWAP, imbalance, absorption, and where the smart money actually sits.

04

Trading psychology

Ego, fear, FOMO, and the habits that quietly destroy accounts — plus a framework to disarm them.

05

Live execution labs

Two live sessions each week — you trade, you journal, you review with a mentor.

06

Building your own system

Backtesting, forward testing, and the discipline to keep a playbook you actually trust.
