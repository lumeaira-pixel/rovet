Our Craft

Slow perfume,
made with intent.

We believe fragrance is memory made tangible. Every Fazanish composition begins with a single raw material — wild-harvested, cold-pressed, or steam-distilled — and is built outward in layers, never rushed.

  • Sustainably sourced, fully traceable ingredients
  • Vegan, cruelty-free, and phthalate-free
  • Recyclable glass and compostable packaging
