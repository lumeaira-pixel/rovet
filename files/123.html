About Glomins

Glomins was born in the heart of Valanchery with a simple idea: cosmetics should be gentle, effective, and joyful. We blend modern skincare science with time‑tested botanical extracts to create products that celebrate every skin tone.

Every item is thoughtfully formulated — free from parabens and cruelty, packed with nourishing ingredients. Whether you're prepping for a big day or enjoying a self‑care evening, Glomins is your trusted glow partner.

We're proud to be a local brand with a global vision, bringing clean beauty to your doorstep.
