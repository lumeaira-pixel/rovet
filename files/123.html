About Glomins

Born in the heart of Valanchery, Glomins is a homegrown cosmetics brand dedicated to clean, effective beauty. We blend traditional botanicals with modern skincare science to create products that are gentle, cruelty-free, and kind to your skin.

Every Glomins creation is crafted in small batches — ensuring freshness, purity, and a personal touch that mass-produced brands can’t replicate. From radiant glow serums to velvet lip tints, we believe beauty should feel as good as it looks.

“Pure botanicals. Honest beauty. That’s the Glomins promise.”
