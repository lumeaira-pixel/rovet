About Glomins

Born in the heart of Valanchery, Glomins is a homegrown cosmetics brand that blends nature’s finest ingredients with modern beauty science. We believe makeup should enhance, not mask.

Every product is cruelty-free, paraben-free, and thoughtfully created for Indian skin tones. From silky foundations to lip tints and skincare essentials — we craft small batches with love.
