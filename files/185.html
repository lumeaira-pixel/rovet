Our Story

Spring 2019

How We Met

A chance encounter at a tiny bookshop in Florence. Olivia was reaching for a poetry collection; James was reaching for the same one. A conversation over espresso turned into hours, and neither of us wanted it to end.

Summer 2020

Our First Date

A picnic by the Arno, complete with a basket of peaches, a bottle of Chianti, and a sky that turned every shade of gold. We talked until the stars came out and knew something rare had begun.

Autumn 2024

The Proposal

On a quiet morning at the Boboli Gardens, with the city waking up below, James knelt in the soft light. The answer was yes before he even finished asking.

November 2026

The day we say "I do" surrounded by the people we love most. We can't wait to celebrate with you in the heart of Tuscany.
