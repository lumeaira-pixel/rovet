How it began

Our Story

The First Meeting

A rainy Tuesday in October

We met at a tiny bookshop in the city — Olivia was reaching for the last copy of a poetry collection, and Ethan happened to grab it at the same moment. We shared a laugh, then a coffee, and the rest became history.

The First Date

Sunset by the pier

We walked along the waterfront, talked for hours about everything and nothing, and ended the night with gelato under a sky full of stars. We both knew this was something special.

The Proposal

A question under the stars

On a quiet evening in the hills, with the city lights twinkling below, Ethan got down on one knee. Olivia said yes before he could even finish the question. It was perfect.

"And now, we invite you to share in our joy as we say 'I do' — surrounded by the people we love most."
