Our Love Story

First Meeting

A Chance Encounter

It was a quiet afternoon in the city library when Nasrin reached for the same book as Shuhaib. Their hands touched, their eyes met, and in that moment, the world stood still. What began as a borrowed novel turned into borrowed hours, then borrowed days — until neither could imagine a life without the other.

The Proposal

She Said Yes

Under a canopy of stars, with the city lights twinkling below, Shuhaib got down on one knee. The ring caught the moonlight as he asked the question that would change everything. Through happy tears, Nasrin whispered yes — and the stars seemed to shine just a little brighter that night.

Now, surrounded by the love of family and friends, Nasrin and Shuhaib invite you to witness the beginning of their greatest adventure. Join them as they exchange vows, share their first kiss as husband and wife, and step into a lifetime of love, laughter, and togetherness.
