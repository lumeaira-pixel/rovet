About

A designer who reads the brief twice.

EV

Elena Voss

Founder & Design Lead

I started my studio after a decade inside product teams at agencies and startups across Amsterdam, Berlin, and London. That vantage point taught me something simple: great design isn't decoration — it's the shortest path between what a business believes and what a customer feels.

Today I work with a small number of clients each year. Founders who want their brand to carry weight. Teams who need a design system that survives contact with reality. I lead every engagement personally, from first sketch to final handoff — no account managers, no telephone game.

Brand strategy first

Every visual decision traces back to a positioning choice.

Systems, not one-offs

Deliverables your team can actually extend without me.

Senior, end to end

One person accountable from kickoff to launch.

Craft as default

Kerning, contrast, and load time are not afterthoughts.
