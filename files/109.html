About NovaCart

A curated marketplace built for resellers & shoppers

NovaCart India is a curated reselling and dropshipping store — not a warehouse. We partner with a vetted network of authorized suppliers who ship products directly to your customers when their terms allow.

Every order is manually reviewed and placed with the supplier to ensure accuracy. Our admin dashboard gives you complete control over pricing, supplier costs, and estimated profit — so you always know your numbers.
