About NovaCart

Reseller-first, customer-obsessed

NovaCart India is a curated reselling store — not a warehouse. We partner with approved suppliers to bring you premium fashion, jewellery, beauty and home products at honest prices. Every product is handpicked, every order is manually verified, and every price includes our transparent margin.

We don't claim direct delivery unless our supplier confirms it. We don't mark orders as paid without server-side verification. What you see is what you get.

Shipping

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Delivery typically takes 3–7 business days depending on your location. Tracking is shared once the supplier dispatches.

Returns & Refunds

7-day return window from delivery. Products must be unused and in original packaging. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after inspection. Certain items like beauty products are non-returnable for hygiene reasons.

Payments

We accept UPI, cards, net banking, and wallets via Razorpay/Cashfree. COD is available on select pin codes after fulfilment setup. All payments are server-side verified.
