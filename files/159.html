What We Offer

Documentation with devotion.

Three ways to preserve the day — each handled with the same quiet care.

The Ceremony

Full documentation of the baptism mass — from the entrance procession to the final blessing. Two photographers, silent and unobtrusive.

The Portraits

Gentle, guided portraits with the child, parents, and godparents in the church or a location of your choosing. Natural light, timeless edits.

The Heirloom Album

A hand-bound linen album, letterpressed with the child's name and date. Designed to be passed down — not scrolled past.
