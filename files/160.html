Our Story

Autumn 2019

A serendipitous meeting

In a quiet bookshop café in Bandra, Aarav reached for the same copy of Rumi's poems as Ananya. What began as a polite exchange became a three-hour conversation about love, poetry, and the meaning of home.

Spring 2023

A promise under the stars

On a rooftop terrace in Udaipur, surrounded by marigolds and the soft glow of lanterns, Aarav knelt and asked Ananya to spend forever with him. She said yes before he could finish the question.

"Two souls, one journey — and a lifetime of shared chapters yet to be written."
