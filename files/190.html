What we do

End-to-end sourcing, built for brands that care.

We don't just find fabric — we find the right fabric for your product, your price point, and your customer. Every recommendation comes with mill verification and physical swatches.

Mill Discovery

We maintain direct relationships with over 120 mills across Kerala — from heritage handloom clusters to modern powerloom units — so you get access, not middlemen.

Quality Assurance

Every batch is checked for GSM, shrinkage, colour fastness and weave consistency. You receive test reports before a single metre is shipped to your unit.

Swatch Library

Curated physical swatch sets delivered to your studio. Feel the drape, see the colour in natural light, and make decisions with confidence.

Sustainable Options

GOTS-certified organic cotton, handspun khadi, natural-dye linens and recycled blends — for brands building a responsible supply chain.

Logistics & Delivery

We coordinate pickup from the mill, consolidate orders, and track shipments to your doorstep anywhere in India — with transparent timelines.

Custom Development

Need a specific weave, colour or finish? We work with mills to develop custom fabric from your brief — from 50-metre sample runs to full production.

How it works

From brief to bolt, in four clear steps.

No endless email chains. No guesswork. Just a straightforward process refined over eight years of sourcing for Kerala's best labels.

Book a sourcing call
STEP 01

Share your brief

Tell us the fabric type, GSM, colour direction, quantity and timeline. If you have a reference image or a competitor sample, even better.

STEP 02

We curate & swatch

Within 5–7 working days, you receive a curated swatch set from 2–3 matching mills, with pricing, lead times and minimum order quantities clearly laid out.

STEP 03

Approve & confirm

Once you select a swatch, we negotiate mill pricing on your behalf, confirm the order, and place it with a signed quality agreement.

STEP 04

Quality check & delivery

We inspect the batch at the mill, arrange logistics, and deliver to your unit. You get tracking updates and a final QC report with every shipment.
