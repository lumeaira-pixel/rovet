Our story

Rooted in respect for land and craft.

Terrafood & Minerals began in 1998 as a small cooperative of growers and geologists who believed that what we eat and what we mine should never come at the cost of purity or place.

Today, we work with over 120 producers across 26 countries — from olive groves in the Peloponnese to salt flats in the Algarve and clay deposits in the American Southwest. Every partnership is built on long-term contracts, fair pricing, and regenerative practices.

We don't just sell ingredients. We document their journey — soil to shelf — so chefs, formulators, and families can trust exactly what they're bringing into their kitchens and workshops.
