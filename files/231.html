About Me

Creative. Precise.

I am Mohammed Rashid, a multidisciplinary creative based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. With a foundation in videography, video editing, photography, and graphic design, I approach every project with a commitment to visual excellence and narrative clarity.

My work spans commercial productions, event coverage, automotive features, and brand identity systems. I believe great visuals are not just seen — they are felt. Whether I'm behind the camera, in the editing suite, or building a design system, my goal remains the same: to create work that resonates.
