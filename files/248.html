Our Philosophy

We don't plan weddings.
We compose them.

Éternel was born from a simple belief: that a wedding should feel less like an event and more like a living, breathing work of art. One that reflects the two people at its center — their history, their humor, their quiet rituals.

For over a decade, we've had the privilege of designing celebrations across Tuscany, Provence, the Amalfi Coast, and beyond. No two are ever alike. Because no two love stories ever are.

What We Do

Full-service design,
from first idea to last dance.

Whether you're dreaming of a six-guest elopement in the hills or a three-day celebration under the stars, we handle every detail with the same obsessive care.

Creative Direction

A singular aesthetic vision for your day — palette, textures, florals, lighting, stationery, and the quiet details that make a room feel like you.

Venue Curation

Access to a private list of estates, olive groves, cliffside chapels, and hidden courtyards — many of which never appear on any search engine.

Vendor Curation

We work only with artisans we trust — Michelin-trained chefs, third-generation florists, string quartets, and photographers who shoot in silence.

Full Planning

Logistics, timelines, budgets, contracts, and the hundred small decisions — managed end-to-end so you can actually be present for your own wedding.

Weekend Design

Welcome dinners, rehearsal suppers, morning-after brunches — we design the full arc of the weekend so every moment feels intentional.

Destination Logistics

Guest travel, accommodation blocks, welcome gifts, and on-the-ground coordination in Italy, France, Spain, Greece, and beyond.
