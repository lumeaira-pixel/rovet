Our Story

A love written
in the stars

We met on a rainy afternoon in a small bookshop in Florence. Julian was reaching for a collection of Neruda's poetry; Elena was reaching for the same book. What began as a polite disagreement over who saw it first became a conversation that lasted until the shop closed.

Seven years, three cities, and countless cups of espresso later, we returned to that same bookshop — and Julian proposed on the very spot where we first met. It felt like coming home.

Now, we invite you to join us as we begin our next chapter, surrounded by the rolling hills and golden light of Tuscany.

"The best thing to hold onto in life is each other."
