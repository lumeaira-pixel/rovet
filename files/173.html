Our philosophy

Slow made,
soulfully worn

Founded in 2012, LUMIÈRE began with a simple belief: jewellery should be felt as much as seen. We work with a small circle of master goldsmiths, using age-old techniques and recycled precious metals.

Each piece takes between 40 and 120 hours — not because we're slow, but because we refuse to rush what matters.
